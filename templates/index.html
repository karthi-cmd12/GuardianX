Security First

Your data stays yours.

GuardianX was built around a simple principle: the tool that protects you should never become a new risk.

  • Analysis happens locally. Scans run through GuardianX's built-in rule engine — no third-party AI service receives your content.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. Your scans, contacts and reports are never used for advertising or resold.
  • Uploads are tightly capped. File submissions are limited to 16 MB and landed in a dedicated quarantine area.
  • Full control over records. Clear your scan history or account data at any time from your settings.
